## Usage examples 

(for learners)


AI summary

<!-- Note -->
The first option is to create an "AI summary" from the existing course content.
This one I consider a bit problematic:

* Just restating the content in different words isn't particularly helpful nor conducive to learning.
* Often, unless you're prompting the LLM explicitly to reduce the word count, you end up getting a "summary" that is longer than the original.
* However, if you *do* restrict the output length, you'll often lose content that is quite critical for understanding the material.
* Conversely, you can't prompt the LLM to put the material into a wider context, because you're risking hallucinations.


"AI Teaching Assistant"

(AITA)? <!-- .element class="fragment" -->

<!-- Note -->
The second option --- enabling the learner to interact with the course material via a chatbot --- does have better merit: it actually relieves course authors of having to deal with learner questions that arise from simply not having read the material. (This is more common than you'd think.)
